Standard editor

Quick profile settings: banner, avatar and description. That's enough to start.

How to open it

  1. Go to your profile page.
  2. Click ✏️ Edit (top right).
  3. To the right of the window there is a Standard / ⚡ PRO switch. You need “Standard” now.

What you can change

FieldWhat it does
BannerA wide image above the profile. Best size 1200×480, no more than 4 MB.
AvatarA round image with your name. No more than 4 MB.
DescriptionText about you, up to 2000 characters. The profile shows two lines, the rest opens with “…more”.

How to upload an image

  1. Click Choose file under the field you need and pick an image on your computer.
  2. A preview appears above right away.
  3. Click Save. “Saved” means everything worked.

Until you click Save, nothing changes on the profile. The Open profile button shows how it turned out.

Not working?

What you seeWhat to do
“The file is too large”Compress the image (for example, on squoosh.app) or choose a smaller one. The limit is 4 MB.
“Please choose an image”PNG, JPEG, WebP and GIF are supported.
