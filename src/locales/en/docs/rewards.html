Stream points and rewards

Viewers collect stream points for watching and spend them on your channel's rewards. Set it up in the studio under 🎁 Rewards.

How viewers get points

  • Under the chat of your stream, on the left, is a button with the balance. Every 5 minutes of watching a gift 🎁 appears on its top edge.
  • Click it — +50 SP for followers or +10 SP for others. The bonus has to be clicked: just an open tab collects nothing.
  • The gift appears only during a stream. Time counts from the start of the stream: minutes spent on the page before it don't count. When the stream ends, the gift disappears right away.
  • The server checks that the viewer really was on the page for all 5 minutes, so scripts can't farm points.
  • Every channel has its own balance. You don't collect points on your own channel.

Name and icon

On the right of the “Rewards” page is the “Channel stream points” block. The name can be changed once every 30 days — that's the only limit; everything else (icon, rewards, prices, texts) you can change as often as you like. The “+50 SP” pop-up always says “SP”, even with a custom name.

Create and edit a reward

➕ Create reward opens the editor (up to 30 rewards). To change an existing one — ✏️ Edit in its card or ✏️ next to it in the list on the right (this works for a reward that is off, too). In the editor:

  • Icon — a colored square with the first letter of the title or your own photo (⟳ in the corner).
  • Title, cost, background color and description — what the viewer gets. The viewer sees the description when choosing the reward.
  • “Viewer writes a text” — when buying, the viewer enters a message. It goes to the “Reward” event, the API and the bot reply.
  • Bot reply in chat — optional. The bot posts it in chat right after a purchase. Under the field are the variables: click one and it's inserted where the cursor is. Below you can see how the reply will look in chat.

Changes are saved with the Save button: if the title has a mistake or the cost is wrong, the reward isn't broken — the editor shows what's wrong.

Bot reply variables

VariableReplaced with
{user}The buyer's name
{reward}The reward title
{cost}The cost
{message}The viewer's text (if “Viewer writes a text” is on)
{points}The channel's stream points name
{balance}How many points the viewer has left after the purchase

For example: 🎵 {user} requests: {message} → “🎵 viewer123 requests: Nirvana — Lithium”. The channel's word filter applies to the bot reply too.

Cards and checkboxes

  • The checkbox on the right turns a reward on and off: viewers don't see a reward that is off.
  • The card shows how the reward looks, the bot reply, 📋 API (the reward address for your bot), 📤 Publish and 🗑 Delete.
  • 📤 Publish shares the reward in “Presets” so other streamers can add it. Starter rewards and ones similar to them can't be published — every channel already has them.

How a viewer buys

  • Clicks the balance button — the rewards window opens: three in a row, the rest scrolls.
  • Clicks a reward — sees the description, the cost and, if needed, a text field. Buy for … spends the points. Not enough — the button is disabled and shows how many more are needed.
  • Someone banned in the channel's chat or in a timeout can't buy rewards.

Starter rewards

Every channel already has them — you can turn them off, edit or delete them:

RewardWhat it does
Highlight my message (100)The viewer's message appears in chat as a colored bar — on the page and in the OBS chat overlay.
Heads or tails (50)The bot flips a coin and posts the result in chat.
Hello on screen (200)The viewer's text shows in OBS through the “🎁 Reward” event (turn it on in “Events”).

“Highlight my message” and “Hello on screen” always need a text: that checkbox can't be turned off for them.

Purchases

The 🧾 Purchases card shows recent purchases. Did what was asked — click ✓ Done. Can't — ↩ Refund points, and the viewer gets the whole price back. Purchases are kept for 30 days.
