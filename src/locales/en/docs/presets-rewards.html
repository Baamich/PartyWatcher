Reward presets

Ready-made stream point rewards from other streamers. Open 🎨 Presets and pick the “Rewards” filter.

Add one

  • The card shows how the reward looks, its cost, description and an example of the bot reply in chat.
  • ＋ Add — the reward appears in your “Rewards”, already on. Then you can change anything: title, cost, description, bot reply.
  • Removing the preset from added ones (the bin in the card) also deletes the reward from your rewards.
  • A channel can have up to 30 rewards — if there's no room, delete one first.

A reward brings its limits (cooldown, per-stream limits, “only while live”) and its OBS widget action along. If you have no widget with that command name, add a matching one from the widget presets or pick another one in the reward editor.

Share your own

  • In “Rewards”, click 📤 Publish in your reward's card, or in “Presets” — ＋ Publish preset → “Reward”.
  • The title, description, cost, color, “viewer writes a text” and the bot reply are published. Your own reward photo isn't — others get a colored square with a letter.
  • Starter rewards (“Highlight my message”, “Heads or tails”, “Hello on screen”) and ones similar to them can't be published — every channel already has them.
  • Likes, sorting and deleting work like for styles and commands.
