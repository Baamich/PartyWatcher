Chat builder

Set up the chat look for OBS without code: choose colors and size, watch the result on the right and copy a ready link.

How to open it

  1. Open the studio and click 🧩 Builder (in the menu on the left, above “Docs”).
  2. In the narrow column on the right choose 💬 Chat.

What you can change

SettingWhat it does
Ready-made stylesButtons at the top: Classic, Dark plates, Glass, Neon, Gradient, Outline, Minimal, Flat bar, Ribbon, Cyberpunk, Pastel, Retro. Click one and all settings are set for you; then you can tweak them.
Chat viewList — messages one above another. Flat bar — one full-width message at the bottom, a new one replaces the old one. Ribbon — messages in one line.
Position and alignmentBottom or top of the screen; left, center or right; max message width; opacity of the whole chat.
TextSize, a font from your computer, weight, italic, letter spacing, line height, color, shadow and letter outline.
Text colorA palette circle or a field: you can enter #ffe066 or rgb(255,224,102).
Background, gradient, blurTurn on “Background under a message”: color, opacity, a second color for the gradient and blur under the plate appear.
ShapeCorner radius, inner padding, border, a colored stripe on the left, glow around a message.
NicknamesThe plate on your nickname, colorful viewer nicknames, nickname on a colored plate, NICKNAMES IN CAPS, showing the BOT and TWITCH labels.
AnimationSlide in, pop, fade in or no animation. The “Play animation” button plays it in the preview.
Disappearing and countAfter how many seconds a message disappears and how many fit on screen (5–100).

How to use it

  1. Move the settings. The Preview on the right shows the result immediately.
  2. Click “Copy OBS link”. The chat key is added automatically (if there is none yet, the site offers to create it).
  3. In OBS open the “Browser” source with the chat, paste the new link into the URL field and click OK. How to add a source is described in Chat in OBS.

Your settings are remembered in this browser. The “Reset settings” button brings everything back.

The link contains the chat key. Don't show it on stream and don't send it to others. More: Security and limits.
