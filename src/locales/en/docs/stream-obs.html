Streaming from OBS

You set up OBS once. After that going live is a single button.

What you need beforehand

  • OBS Studio. Free download: obsproject.com.
  • An account on partywatcher.de and your streamer profile. For example, Baamich's is here: https://partywatcher.de/streamers/baamich.

Step 1. Open the “Creator studio”

Go to your profile page and click 🛠️ Creator studio (top right). Or open it directly: https://partywatcher.de/workbench.html.

Step 2. Get a stream key

  1. In the studio find the “Stream key” window.
  2. Click “🔄 Generate”. Dots will appear in the field: that's the hidden key.
  3. Click 📋 next to the field. The whole key is copied. Nothing else to do.

Step 3. Put the key into OBS

  1. In OBS click Settings (bottom right), then choose Stream on the left.
  2. Service: choose Custom….
  3. Server: paste rtmp://live.partywatcher.de/live. You can copy it in the studio: it is written under the key, click it.
  4. Stream Key: paste the copied key (Ctrl+V).
  5. Click Apply, then OK.

Step 4. Set up the quality (once)

  1. In OBS: Settings → Output. At the top set Output Mode: Advanced, the Streaming tab opens.
  2. Encoder: NVENC H.264 if you have an NVIDIA graphics card. Otherwise x264.
  3. Rate Control: CBR.
  4. Bitrate: 4000. If your internet is weak, use 2500.
  5. Keyframe Interval: 2.
  6. Click OK.

How to choose the bitrate: open speedtest.net and look at Upload. The bitrate should not exceed 70% of that number (in Kbps).

Step 5. Go live

  1. In OBS click Start Streaming.
  2. In the studio, in the “Stream” window, “Offline” changes to “Live” and a timer starts. This takes a few seconds.
  3. Open your profile https://partywatcher.de/streamers/baamich (put your name at the end). The stream turns on by itself.

Not working?

What you seeWhat to do
OBS says “Failed to connect to server”Check that the address is written exactly as above, without spaces, and that the whole key is pasted.
The studio keeps showing “Offline”Wait 10 seconds. If that doesn't help, click “Stop Streaming” in OBS and start again.
The stream dropped after “🔄 Generate”That's expected: the old key stops working immediately. Put the new key into OBS.
The studio shows “Reconnecting”Bad internet or too high a bitrate. Lower it (2500).

Don't show the key on stream or in screenshots. Anyone who knows the key can stream on your channel. If the key leaked, click “🔄 Generate”: the old one stops working.
