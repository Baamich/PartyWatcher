Channel activity

Moderators, their permissions, banned viewers and followers — in the studio under 🛡️ Channel activity.

Moderators

  • Enter the account login (not the streamer name), choose a level and click Assign. Up to 50 moderators.
  • A moderator (green axe badge) watches the chat.
  • A senior moderator (orange shield with an axe) can also manage the stream: change the title and category, start a raid.
  • The level is changed in the list, ✕ removes a moderator. Changes reach an open chat right away, without reloading.

Permissions

In the “Moderator permissions” card, tick what each level can do. Untick a box and moderators of that level lose that button in chat.

PermissionWhere it works
Delete messages🗑️ next to a chat message
Timeout, Ban⏱️ and 🚫 next to a message
Clear the whole chat🧹 in the chat header
Chat settings⚙ in the chat header: followers only, slow mode, word filter
Title and description, category, raid🛠 “Stream management” in the chat header — senior only (🔒 for regular moderators)

Only lower ranks can be punished: a moderator can't ban a moderator, a senior can't ban a senior, and nobody can touch you. “Followers only” and slow mode don't apply to you and your moderators.

Chat commands

You and your moderators don't have to look for buttons: type a command right in the chat field — on the channel page or in the studio. Type / and a list of the commands you're allowed to use appears above the field (a moderator sees only the ones their permissions allow). ↑/↓ — choose, Tab or Enter — insert, then add the name. Commands don't go to chat; the result is shown only to you.

CommandWhat it doesPermission
/ban name, /unban nameBan permanently / lift the banBan
/timeout name [time], /untimeout nameMute for a while / lift the timeout. Time: 90 (seconds), 10m, 2h, 1d; none — 10 minutesTimeout
/clearClear the whole chatClear chat
/slow [seconds], /slowoffSlow mode (3, 5, 10, 30, 60 or 120 s; no number — 30) / turn offChat settings
/followers [time], /followersoffFollowers only (10m, 30m, 1h, 1d; no time — right after following) / turn offChat settings
/filter 0–4Word filter: 0 — off, 4 — maximumChat settings
/raid channel [seconds], /unraidRaid another stream (in 15 s, 5–120 allowed) / cancelRaid
/title textChange the stream titleTitle

The name can be written with @ and in any case. The rank rule is the same as for the buttons: a moderator can't ban a moderator.

Action log

Everything your moderators and you do is written to the “Action log” on the main studio page: bans and unbans, timeouts and lifting them, deleted messages, clearing the chat, chat mode changes (followers only, slow mode, filter), raids and cancelling them, changes of the stream title, description and category. You see who did it and when. Entries are kept for 7 days.

Banned

Everyone banned in chat. Unban lets the person write again.

Followers

The total number of followers and the latest 100 — with the follow date.
