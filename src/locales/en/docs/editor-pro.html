Pro editor

In PRO mode you arrange everything on the page yourself: move and resize blocks, add text, shapes, photos and link buttons. Viewers see the page exactly as you left it.

How to open it

  1. Open ✏️ Edit on your profile.
  2. Click ⚡ PRO to the right of the window. A full-screen editor opens.
  3. Leave with the 👤 To profile button. If something is unsaved, the site will ask.

What's on the screen

  • At the top the tools: 🖱️ select, ✂️ scissors, T text, 📎 photo, 🔗 link button. On the right ↶ ↷, “Reset layout”, “Save” and “Close”.
  • On the left the modes: Profile, Live and two 📱 modes to see how it looks on a phone.
  • In the center the stage with your blocks. Above it there are counters: how many blocks, photos and buttons you have used.

Move, resize, configure

  • Move: hold a block with the mouse and drag.
  • Resize: drag the square in the bottom-right corner. The size is shown above the block, for example 300×200.
  • Block settings: hover over a block, three dots ⋯ appear at the top right. Click them and a menu opens next to the block. The menu updates by itself when you change something.
  • Delete removes the selected custom block, Esc cancels the tool.

Built-in blocks (banner, avatar, name and description, recordings, player, chat) can't be deleted, and they can be shrunk to no less than 60% of the default. The player shrinks by at most 10%, the chat can't be narrower than the default width, and the chat height equals the player height.

Layers: what's on top of what

Each block's three-dot menu has ⬆ Bring to front and ⬇ Send to back. The avatar has no layer buttons: it always stays above the banner. On the live page photo blocks always stay in the background.

Tools

✂️ Scissors: your own shapes

  1. Click ✂️ and choose a shape: circle, square, triangle or freehand.
  2. The whole stage turns translucent white. Hold the mouse and drag out a shape. For freehand, trace the outline you want; it closes by itself when you release.
  3. A new block appears. In its menu you can choose a fill or put a photo inside the shape.

There are no scissors in “Live” mode.

T Text

Click T, hold the mouse and drag out an area, like in Word. The three dots have many settings: font, size, text and background colors, opacity, bold, italic, underline, alignment, corner radius, border, shadow.

📎 Photo

Choose an image, it becomes a separate block. On the live page a photo is only a background. Photos are compressed automatically (GIF animation is lost).

🔗 Link button

  1. Click 🔗, a button appears. It doesn't need a photo.
  2. Open the three dots and fill in the Label and the Link (only http:// or https://).
  3. Set the button color, text color and size, shape and corner radius. Optionally add a photo, it becomes the button background.

Any block can become a button: shapes, photos and text have 🔗 Make a button in their three dots, and a button has ▢ Make a regular block. Only a freehand shape can't become a button.

How to show a photo in a block

When a block has a photo (banner, avatar, shape, photo, button), the three dots show 🎯 How to show the photo. The window shows the block's shape and the dimmed part of the photo around it, which won't be in the block.

  • Drag the photo to choose which part to show. The mouse wheel or the slider changes the zoom.
  • Fill covers the whole block (the excess is cropped), Whole shows the entire photo with margins, Stretch stretches it to the block size.
  • If the proportions match, the window says there is no cropping.

Recordings: horizontal or vertical

The “Recordings” block's three dots have a ↕ Show vertically button (and ↔ Show horizontally to go back). Vertically the recordings go in one column, horizontally in a grid.

Modes

ModeWhat it is
ProfileThe profile page: banner, avatar, description, recordings and all your stuff. Stage width 1100.
LiveThe player and chat visible while the stream is open. The stage is wider (1600) to have room. You can move the player and chat, add text, buttons and a photo background.
Profile 📱View only: how the profile looks on a phone (scaled down).
Live 📱View only: on a phone the live page is always standard, player on top and chat below. A custom live layout works on screens from 900 pixels.

Undo and redo

The ↶ and ↷ buttons at the top right (or Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y). Up to 60 steps are kept. If the cursor is in an input field, Ctrl+Z undoes typing in that field.

Saving and resetting

  • 💾 Save sends the layout to the site. Until you save, viewers see nothing new.
  • ↺ Reset layout removes all your blocks and returns the built-in ones to their places. This is saved immediately.
