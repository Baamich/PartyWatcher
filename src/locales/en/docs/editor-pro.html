Pro editor

In PRO mode you arrange everything on the page yourself: move and resize blocks, add text, shapes, photos and link buttons. Viewers see the page exactly as you left it.

How to open it

  1. Open ✏️ Edit on your profile.
  2. Click PRO to the right of the window. A full-screen editor opens.
  3. Leave with the 👤 To profile button. If something is unsaved, the site will ask.

What's on the screen

  • At the top the tools: 🖱️ select, ✂️ scissors, T text, 📎 photo, 🔗 link button, 🌄 page background. On the right ↶ ↷, “Reset layout”, “Save” and “Close”.
  • On the left the modes: Profile, Live and two 📱 modes to see how it looks on a phone.
  • In the center the stage with your blocks. Above it there are counters: how many blocks, photos, buttons and backgrounds you have used (with a GIF there is also a “GIF n/2” counter). Hover a counter and those blocks light up white right on the stage. Click it and the highlight stays, in blue (click again to remove it).

Move, resize, configure

  • Move: hold a block with the mouse and drag.
  • Resize: drag the square in the bottom-right corner. The size is shown above the block, for example 300×200.
  • Block settings: hover over a block, three dots ⋯ appear at the top right. Click them and a menu opens next to the block. The menu updates by itself when you change something.
  • Delete removes the selected custom block, Esc cancels the tool.

Built-in blocks (banner, avatar, name and description, recordings, player, chat) can't be deleted, and they can be shrunk to no less than 60% of the default. The player shrinks by at most 10%, the chat can't be narrower than the default width, and the chat height equals the player height.

Layers: what's on top of what

Each block's three-dot menu has ⬆ Bring to front and ⬇ Send to back. The avatar has no layer buttons: it always stays above the banner. On the live page photo blocks always stay in the background.

Tools

✂️ Scissors: your own shapes

  1. Click ✂️ and choose a shape: circle, square, triangle or freehand.
  2. The whole stage turns translucent white. Hold the mouse and drag out a shape. For freehand, trace the outline you want; it closes by itself when you release.
  3. A new block appears. In its menu you can choose a fill or put a photo inside the shape.

There are no scissors in “Live” mode.

T Text

Click T, hold the mouse and drag out an area, like in Word. The three dots have many settings: font, size, text and background colors, opacity, bold, italic, underline, alignment, corner radius, border, shadow.

📎 Photo

Choose an image, it becomes a separate block. On the live page a photo is only a background. Photos are compressed automatically. GIFs stay animated: up to 1.2 MB and no more than 2 for the whole design (including background and live).

🔗 Link button

  1. Click 🔗, a button appears. It doesn't need a photo.
  2. Open the three dots and fill in the Label and the Link (only http:// or https://).
  3. Set the button color, text color and size, shape and corner radius. Optionally add a photo, it becomes the button background.

Any block can become a button: shapes, photos and text have 🔗 Make a button in their three dots, and a button has ▢ Make a regular block. Only a freehand shape can't become a button.

🌄 Page background

The background lies under all profile blocks. The Stretch over the whole page checkbox in the background menu stretches it for viewers over the whole page below the site header, not just the profile area: the transition stays at the same spot relative to the blocks as on the stage, and a photo is pinned to the window like the sides — on a long profile it doesn’t stretch or run out. Without the checkbox the background is only under the profile. Press 🌄 at the top (only in the “Profile” mode). While the background menu is open, blocks become semi-transparent so you can see what changes. A GIF works as a background too.

  1. ＋ Add background — pick a color or press 📷 Photo as background. A photo can be shown as Fill (the whole page, extra is cropped), Whole or Tiled.
  2. ＋ Second background (transition) — add a second color or photo. 2 backgrounds at most.
  3. Set up the transition between them:
    • Direction: ↕ top to bottom or ↔ left to right.
    • Where: a slider from 0 to 100%. While the background menu is open, a dashed line on the stage shows the transition.
    • Softness: how stretched the transition is (0 — sharp, 50 — very smooth).
    • Effect: how exactly one background turns into the other.

Page sides

In the same 🌄 menu, below the background, there are sides: left and right of the profile, the full window height. They don’t scroll with the page, like a stream channel’s artwork.

  • ＋ Left side / ＋ Right side — a color first, then 📷 Photo on the side if you like (a GIF works too). The second side starts with the first side’s color and photo; change it if needed.
  • Photo size: the menu suggests a photo size for a 1920×1080 screen at the current profile width (for example, about 400×1080 for an 1100-wide profile). On other screens the photo adjusts: Fill crops the extra, Whole shows all of it, Tiled repeats it.
  • The wider the profile, the narrower the sides. With a profile almost full width (1760) the sides barely show. There are no sides on phones.

👁 View on the page

The full-page background and the sides show only on the profile page itself. The 👁 View on the page button in the background menu opens your profile in a new tab with your unsaved edits, and it follows them as you edit. A “Preview” label shows at the bottom. Viewers see the changes only after 💾 Save.

Page size

  • Width: the editor has room up to 1760, which fits a browser window on a 1920 screen with margins. For viewers the page is as wide as the rightmost block, but not narrower than 1100 (live: not narrower than 1600). A dashed line with a number on the stage shows the current width: drag a block past it and the profile gets wider. Or set the width right away: 🌄 → Page → Width (by blocks, 1280, 1440, 1600 or 1760 — full screen); then the room on the right appears even with no blocks there yet. Old profiles stay 1100 wide and old live layouts 1600, as they were. On narrower screens everything scales down proportionally.
  • Height: the page grows down as long as there are blocks, up to 10,000. Blocks can't go past the top, left or right edge of the stage.
  • On small screens the whole page scales down to fit the screen width.

How to show a photo in a block

When a block has a photo (banner, avatar, shape, photo, button), the three dots show 🎯 How to show the photo. The window shows the block's shape and the dimmed part of the photo around it, which won't be in the block.

  • Drag the photo to choose which part to show. The mouse wheel or the slider changes the zoom.
  • Fill covers the whole block (the excess is cropped), Whole shows the entire photo with margins, Stretch stretches it to the block size.
  • If the proportions match, the window says there is no cropping.

Recordings: horizontal or vertical

The “Recordings” block's three dots have a ↕ Show vertically button (and ↔ Show horizontally to go back). Vertically the recordings go in one column, horizontally in a grid.

Modes

ModeWhat it is
ProfileThe profile page: banner, avatar, description, recordings, background and all your stuff. Room on the stage up to 1760 wide and up to 10,000 high (see “Page size”).
LiveThe player and chat visible while the stream is open. The stage is up to 1760 wide (by the right edge of the blocks, at least 1600). You can move the player and chat, add text, buttons and a photo background.
Profile 📱View only: how the profile looks on a phone (scaled down). The wider the profile, the smaller it is on a phone — if phone viewers matter to you, don't stretch blocks to 1760 without a reason.
Live 📱View only: on a phone the live page is always standard — player on top, chat below filling the rest of the screen; turn the phone sideways and the video takes the full height with the chat on the right. A custom live layout works on screens from 900 pixels.

Undo and redo

The ↶ and ↷ buttons at the top right (or Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y). Up to 60 steps are kept. If the cursor is in an input field, Ctrl+Z undoes typing in that field.

Saving and resetting

  • 💾 Save sends the layout to the site. Until you save, viewers see nothing new.
  • ↺ Reset layout removes all your blocks and returns the built-in ones to their places. This is saved immediately.

The avatar and banner are always static: a GIF there becomes a regular image. To find where the blocks from a limit are, hover its counter above the stage.
