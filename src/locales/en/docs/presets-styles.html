Chat styles

A style preset is a ready-made set of chat look settings for OBS. You can take someone else's style in one click or share your own.

Where to find them

  1. Open the studio and click 🎨 Presets (in the menu on the left, below “Builder”).
  2. Choose the Styles filter at the top.
  3. The list can be sorted: most liked, most added or newest. There is also a search by name.

How to add someone's style

  1. The card shows a preview: that's how the chat will look. The name and description are below it.
  2. Click ＋ Add and confirm. The style is applied right away in the Chat builder.
  3. Open the Builder, tweak the settings if you like and click “Copy OBS link”.

An added style replaces the current Builder settings. If you don't want to lose your own style, publish it as a preset first (see below): it will stay in your list.

How to publish your style

  1. Set up the chat in the Builder.
  2. Below the preview click 💾 Save as preset (or ＋ Publish preset on the presets page).
  3. Enter a name (required, 3–40 characters) and, if you want, a description (up to 200 characters).
  4. Click Publish. The style appears in the shared list and in the Your presets block.

The style currently set in the Builder is published. A style that doesn't differ from the default can't be published. You can keep up to 20 of your own presets.

Likes and adds

  • 👥 Adds show how many people took the preset.
  • ♥ Likes: one like per account. Clicking again removes the like. Accounts older than one day can like.
  • You can't like or add your own preset.
  • If someone removes the preset from their list, the add counter goes down.

How to delete

  • Your own preset: the trash can at the top right of the card. The site asks “Are you sure you want to delete the preset?”. It disappears from the shared list, but those who already added the style keep their settings.
  • Someone else's, added: the same trash can or the “✓ Added” button removes it from your list. The Builder settings don't change.
  • The easiest way to find a preset is the search by name or the Your presets block at the bottom of the page (it is in the Builder too).

Not working?

What you seeWhat to do
“This style does not differ from the default one”Change the settings in the Builder first, then publish.
“Likes become available one day after signing up”Likes unlock 24 hours after signing up.
“Too often”Wait a minute and try again.
The style was added but the chat looks the sameReopen the Builder (Ctrl+F5) and copy a new OBS link: the old link keeps the old settings.
